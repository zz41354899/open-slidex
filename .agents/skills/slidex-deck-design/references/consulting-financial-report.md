# Consulting and financial report blueprint

Use this route for a 24-32 page board update, earnings review, investment memo,
consulting recommendation, or financial weekly. Thirty pages is a useful
planning target, not a quota. Remove pages that do not earn a decision.

Inspect [the complete native reference](consulting-financial-report.mdx) for
all 30 editable pages. Its figures are illustrative and must be replaced with
verified project evidence.

## Thirty-page decision arc

1. Cover: period, entity, decision context.
2. Executive message: one conclusion and three supporting signals.
3. Decision agenda: what the audience must approve, understand, or monitor.
4. Reporting perimeter: scope, dates, currency, actual versus forecast.
5. Headline KPI scorecard.
6. Revenue versus plan and prior period.
7. Margin bridge or cost driver.
8. Cash, runway, or liquidity position.
9. Segment or product mix.
10. Geography or channel mix.
11. Customer concentration or cohort movement.
12. Operating driver tree.
13. Volume, price, mix, or utilization decomposition.
14. Leading indicators.
15. Benchmark or peer comparison.
16. Balance-sheet movement.
17. Working-capital movement.
18. Capital allocation or investment pacing.
19. Forecast baseline.
20. Upside scenario.
21. Downside scenario.
22. Sensitivity: the two variables that change the answer.
23. Risk heatmap with evidence and owner.
24. Strategic options with explicit trade-offs.
25. Recommendation and why alternatives lose.
26. Ninety-day action plan.
27. Owners, milestones, and decision gates.
28. Monitoring dashboard and trigger thresholds.
29. Method, sources, definitions, and limitations.
30. Closing decision or next meeting commitment.

## Adapt by use case

- Consulting: emphasize diagnosis, options, recommendation, workplan, and owner.
- Investment: emphasize thesis, catalysts, variant view, downside, valuation
  inputs, and falsification triggers. Do not present a recommendation without
  source dates and risk boundaries.
- Earnings: emphasize actual versus plan versus prior period, bridges, guidance,
  cash, and management actions.
- Financial weekly: compress stable background; show deltas, exceptions,
  thresholds, owners, and what changed since the previous issue.

## Evidence rules

- Every number has a period, unit, currency when relevant, source, and status:
  actual, forecast, estimate, or scenario.
- Use a native `Chart` when shape communicates the conclusion and a native
  `Table` when exact lookup matters. Never redraw a financial chart with shapes.
- A waterfall or bridge must reconcile start, drivers, and end. A scenario page
  must identify changed assumptions rather than merely recolor one forecast.
- Separate observed facts, analysis, recommendation, and uncertainty visually.
- Preserve negative signs, zeros, missing values, denominators, and rounding.

## Visual rhythm

Use a stable reporting shell without making every page identical. Rotate among
hero metric, bridge, annotated trend, small multiples, matrix, full-width table,
process, recommendation, and quiet section reset. Keep appendix density out of
the decision arc unless the exact table is required to decide.

For a requested animated deck, route next to `slidex-motion-direction` and read
`references/long-deck-motion.md`. Every page receives at least a deliberate
transition; only evidence that benefits from sequence receives additional
entrances or chart motion.
